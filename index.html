YOUR QUICK BRIEFING

You’ve got this, pilot.

Destroy 10,000 enemies to complete all ten sectors. Firing and enemy waves double every 2,000 kills, reaching 32× speed. Your spread grows to eleven lanes.

  • Move: Drag anywhere in the field with a finger or mouse. Keyboard pilots can use WASD or the arrow keys.
  • Fire: Automatic. Concentrate on dodging.
  • Nova: Tap the NOVA button or press Space to clear enemies and bullets. You get one back every 100 kills, up to three.
  • Shield: You start with five hits. Collect plus pickups to repair.
  • Pause: Tap pause or press P / Esc. Switching apps pauses the game automatically.

On mobile, start your drag below your ship for a clear view. Sound is optional.
